About FlowTask

A small team, one product, and a strong opinion about status meetings.

FlowTask started in 2023 as an internal board for a six-person agency that had outgrown a shared spreadsheet and could not face another migration. Two years later it is the only thing that team runs its week on, and around four thousand other teams do the same.

What we build

One product: a task board with automations and reporting attached. We do not sell a chat app, a wiki or a time tracker, and we integrate with the ones you already pay for instead of asking you to replace them.

How we work

Everything ships behind a flag, every change is reversible for thirty days, and support is answered by the people who wrote the code. There is no tier of the product that unlocks a faster reply.

Where we are

Twelve people across Kyiv, Lisbon and Toronto. Data lives in the EU by default, and Enterprise workspaces can pin it to a region of their choosing.

Talk to us

Write to hello@flowtask.example — we answer within one working day, and the reply comes from a person rather than a queue.

Project management for teams that ship every week.
